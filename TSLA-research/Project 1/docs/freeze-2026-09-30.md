# September 30, 2026 Input and Bridge Freeze

## Freeze convention

- **Valuation date:** September 30, 2026, U.S. regular-market close.
- **Currency and units:** USD millions except per-share values and percentages.
- **Share convention:** FY2025 diluted weighted-average shares of 3,528 million. This is used consistently for DCF per-share conversion, market capitalization, and comparable references. It is not described as September 30 shares outstanding.
- **Enterprise-value bridge:** Enterprise value plus unrestricted cash and short-term investments, less debt and finance leases. Operating leases are excluded because they are not included in the FCFF debt/cost-of-debt convention.
- **Terminal value:** Perpetuity-growth method; terminal growth must remain below WACC.
