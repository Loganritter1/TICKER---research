# Video Runbook

Record only after the model, validation record, and memo are frozen. Keep the webcam visible unless an approved accommodation specifies an equivalent ownership check.

## Video 1 — Research evolution and AI-output check (maximum 3:00)

1. State the decision question and refer to Edition A/Edition B status.
2. Explain the one most consequential change or supported no-change conclusion.
3. Identify whether the driver was course instruction, own analysis, peer challenge, AI, or a mixture.
4. Show one AI-generated claim/suggestion/result and the independent check.
5. State whether it was accepted, qualified, corrected, or rejected and why.

## Video 2 — Code and validation walkthrough (maximum 5:00)

1. Open the submitted repository and show the file map.
2. Explain inputs → pro forma → FCFF → enterprise/equity bridge → output.
3. Show exact clean-run instructions and visible executed output.
4. Demonstrate the highest-risk validation or changed-input test.
5. State one limitation or failure mode.

## Video 3 — Investment committee presentation (maximum 5:00)

1. Demonstrate the final product/model as the main delivery.
2. Lead with the conditional recommendation and valuation range.
3. Defend the strongest evidence, assumptions, and method disagreement.
4. Explain downside risk, reversal conditions, and monitoring triggers.
5. End with the explicit committee ask.
