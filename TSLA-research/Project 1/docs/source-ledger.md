## Definitions policy

- **Reported fact:** Directly reported in an authoritative source.
- **Normalization:** A labeled adjustment to remove, reconcile, or qualify a one-time item or basis change.
- **Forecast assumption:** A forward-looking judgment with a stated basis and challenge.
- **Calculated output:** A model result derived from cited facts and stated assumptions.

All market data must include an as-of date. Do not mix basic shares, diluted weighted-average shares, and current diluted shares without an explicit convention and reconciliation.
